VGG-19 Deep Encoder

Pre-trained normalized VGG-19 extracts deep spatial features (up to relu4_1 layer), separating semantic object content from low-level brush strokes and texture statistics.

Adaptive Instance Norm (AdaIN)

Aligns feature channel mean and variance between content and style: AdaIN(x,y) = σ(y)((x - μ(x))/σ(x)) + μ(y) with zero iterative optimization required at runtime.

Symmetrical Decoder

Trained neural decoder mirrors the VGG layers using nearest-neighbor upsampling and reflection padding to reconstruct the stylized latent representation back into pixel space without artifacts.
